Learn About Cybersecurity

Explore the basic concepts, threats and practices that help people stay safe in the digital world.

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, systems and digital information from unauthorised access, damage and other security threats.

Common Cybersecurity Threats

Understanding common threats can help people recognise suspicious activity and make safer decisions online.

🎣

Phishing

Phishing uses fake emails, messages or websites to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

🦠

Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to damage systems, disrupt operations or gain unauthorised access.

🔑

Password Attacks

Weak or reused passwords can make accounts easier for attackers to access.

Cybersecurity Protection

Good security practices can help protect accounts, devices and personal information.

🔒

Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords for important accounts and avoid reusing the same password.

🔐

Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication adds another verification step to help protect online accounts.

🔄

Software Updates

Keeping software updated can help protect devices against known security vulnerabilities.

Digital Safety

Simple digital safety habits can reduce common security risks when using technology.

🌐

Safe Browsing

Check websites carefully before entering personal information or downloading files.

📧

Check Emails

Be careful with unexpected emails, links and attachments that may contain suspicious content.

🛡️

Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing sensitive information with unknown people or suspicious websites.
