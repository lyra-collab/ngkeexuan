Technology In Action

Discover how cybersecurity is applied in different areas of modern life and the digital workplace.

Cybersecurity in the Real World

Cybersecurity is important in many areas because organisations and individuals rely on digital systems to store, process and share information. The following examples show how cybersecurity helps protect people, information and digital services.

Cybersecurity in Education

Schools, colleges and universities use digital systems for learning, communication and storing student information. Cybersecurity helps protect these systems and the information they contain.

🔐

Student Information

Security measures help protect student records and other sensitive information from unauthorised access.

💻

Online Learning

Secure accounts and learning platforms help students access online classes and resources safely.

🛡️

Account Security

Strong passwords and multi-factor authentication can help protect student and staff accounts.

Cybersecurity in Business

Businesses depend on computers, networks, cloud services and digital information. Cybersecurity helps organisations reduce security risks and protect their operations.

📊

Business Data

Security controls can help protect important business information from unauthorised access.

☁️

Cloud Services

Organisations can use access controls and other security measures to protect cloud-based systems.

👥

Employee Security

Cybersecurity awareness can help employees recognise phishing attempts and other common security threats.

Cybersecurity in Healthcare

Healthcare organisations use digital systems to manage patient information, communication and healthcare services. Cybersecurity is important for protecting these systems and maintaining confidentiality.

🩺

Patient Information

Security measures help protect sensitive patient information from unauthorised access.

💾

Digital Records

Electronic health records require appropriate security controls to help protect stored data.

🔒

Secure Systems

Secure systems can help healthcare organisations maintain reliable access to important digital services.

Cybersecurity Applications

Cybersecurity can be applied differently depending on the needs of each environment.

Area Cybersecurity Application Purpose
Education Account and data protection Protect student and staff information
Business Network and information security Protect business systems and data
Healthcare Patient data protection Protect sensitive healthcare information
